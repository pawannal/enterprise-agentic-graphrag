# Incident INC-001: Neo4j Connectivity Failure

Incident ID: INC-001

Date: August 20, 2026

Severity: High

Project Phoenix experienced a Neo4j connectivity failure during a production test.

The incident affected GraphRAG retrieval because the knowledge graph could not be queried.

The AI Platform Team investigated the issue.

The incident was resolved by restoring the database connection configuration.

Neo4j was the primary technology affected by the incident.

The incident highlighted the need for improved monitoring and connection health checks.

The incident was resolved on August 20, 2026.
