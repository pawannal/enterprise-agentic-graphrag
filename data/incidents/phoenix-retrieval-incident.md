# Incident INC-003: Phoenix Retrieval Quality Issue

Incident ID: INC-003

Date: August 27, 2026

Severity: Medium

Project Phoenix produced incomplete answers for questions requiring information from multiple enterprise documents.

The issue was caused by retrieval returning semantically similar chunks without enough relationship context.

The AI Platform Team investigated the issue.

The team decided to improve retrieval by combining vector search with knowledge graph traversal.

The incident resulted in an enhancement to the GraphRAG retrieval architecture.

The incident was resolved on August 29, 2026.
